Anand sold his vehicle in 2023, but because the transfer never completed, challans for a stranger's driving still appear against his name. Rasta is an independent vehicle compliance passport that shows citizens where they stand, why a challan may be wrong, and what they can do before the easiest button becomes the costliest choice.

The citizen enters a fictional vehicle number or taps a demo vehicle. Rasta shows documents, open challans, clean streak, proposed rewards and the reasons behind the score. When a challan looks wrong, the citizen explains the problem in plain language or by voice. The app classifies the likely ground, shows its reasoning, highlights the action window and camera-record deadline, compares mock evidence, and drafts editable paperwork. The citizen reviews and sends manually; the prototype never auto-submits anything.

The new idea is that disputes and compliance defend each other. A wrong challan puts a clean streak at risk, so the passport gives the citizen a reason to contest it. If the dispute is upheld, the streak is restored retroactively.

All vehicles, challans, owners, rewards, evidence images, accounts and backend responses are synthetic. No government system, real personal data, payment gateway, OTP, official logo or private API is used.
